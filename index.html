📱 Finding 1 · Content Gap

Instagram and TikTok are posting images. Images reach a fraction of the audience that short videos reach. The same content filmed as a 20-second clip gets 5–10x more views.

🔗 Finding 2 · Booking Path

People watch, but there is no clear next step in the post. No "book now" link, no phone number in the caption. Interested viewers leave without taking action.

📡 Finding 3 · Missed Platforms

Snapchat is active but not advertised on. No paid ads anywhere. The 1.7M-view video got there organically — paid ads would reach that audience every week on demand.
